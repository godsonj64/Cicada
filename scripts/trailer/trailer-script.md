# Cicada — 30-Second Trailer

**Tone:** Apple keynote. Calm, certain, human. Black frames. Thin white type. Long
breaths of silence. The product does the talking. No feature dumps — one idea, one
feeling: *the distance between an idea and a working thing just went to zero.*

**Music:** a single sustained piano/pad that swells once, at the pull-back. Silence is a
tool. (Drop a track at `scripts/trailer/music.mp3` and pass `--music` to score it.)

**Format:** 1920×1080 · 30 fps · captions over real product footage. The finished film is
cut *to the voiceover* — the table below is the 30-second reference beat map; the automation
scales the same beats to whatever VO you supply (the bundled ElevenLabs read is ~64s, so the
render breathes longer between the same moments).

## Why this order (the Jobs spine)

1. **Tension first** — name the universal frustration before showing a single pixel.
2. **The turn** — the product appears exactly when the promise is made ("Just describe it").
3. **Proof, not features** — show it *thinking and running*, then a real artifact (a chart).
4. **The value nobody expected** — it's private, and it publishes. The "one more thing" beat.
5. **Rest, then the mark** — pull back so the eye takes in the whole thing, then just the logo.

Keep the words scarce. Every caption is ≤ 5 words. The silence sells it.
